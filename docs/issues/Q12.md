# Q12: Stock is higher than the warehouse count after cancellations

**Reported by:** warehouse operations · **Severity:** high (overselling risk)

The weekly stock count for BAG-TOTE was 6 units lower than OrderHub's stock figure. The mobile app retries `POST /orders/{id}/cancel` when the network times out. Support noticed a few orders where the customer pressed "cancel" twice.

No error was reported to anyone. Orders show status CANCELLED as expected.

**What we know:** the gap appeared after the mobile app release that added automatic retries.
