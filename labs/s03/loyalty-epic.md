# Workshop 3.7: slice the loyalty epic

**Epic:** Customers earn loyalty points on every order (1 point per whole currency unit of the order total), see their balance, and can spend points as a discount on a later order (100 points = 1.00). Points expire after 12 months. Cancelling an order removes the points it earned; returning items removes points in proportion. Finance needs the points liability (unspent points × value) in the nightly billing data.

Slice it into pull requests. For each slice: a title, the behaviour it delivers, the criteria it would cover, a size estimate (keep each under 300 lines), and what it depends on. Mark the slice you'd do last, and the one you'd refuse to start without another conversation.
