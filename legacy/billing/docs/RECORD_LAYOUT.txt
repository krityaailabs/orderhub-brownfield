  pos 1      'H'
  pos 2-7    business date DDMMYY
  pos 8-15   'ORDERHUB'

Detail (D)
  pos 1      'D'
  pos 2-11   order id, zero filled
  pos 12-41  customer name, left aligned, space filled
  pos 42-43  country
  pos 44-46  currency
  pos 47-56  net amount, implied 2 decimals, zero filled
  pos 57-66  tax amount
  pos 67-76  gross amount

Trailer (T)
  pos 1      'T'
  pos 2-7    record count
  pos 8-20   gross total
